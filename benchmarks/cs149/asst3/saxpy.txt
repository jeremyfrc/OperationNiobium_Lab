The data movement actually took longer time to complete, so the BW dropped after consider that part. Limited by PCIe bus real throughput.
Also, BW not matching with T4 whose spec is about 32GB/s, here only 11GB/s.
PCIe 4.0 x8 theoretical limit is 16-32GB/s, local 11GB/s is good enough.
Pageable memory actually is the key to lower down the speed:
int* array = new int[N]; // 这分配的是 Pageable Memory（可分页内存）
当执行 cudaMemcpy 时，由于操作系统可能随时把这段内存交换到硬盘（虚拟内存）里，英伟达驱动为了安全，必须先在 CPU 内存里开辟一块隐秘的、不可分页的锁页内存（Pinned Memory），把你的数据拷贝一次过去，然后再通过 PCIe 发送给 GPU。
